# Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

This privacy policy describes what data the Open Preview Peek browser extension
collects, and how said data is stored and used.

---

## What data is accessed by the Extension

The extension reads certain webpage metadata on the page the extension is opened
on. This only occurs when the extension popup is opened. Until the extension
popup is opened and while in its closed state, the extension does not access any
data whatsoever. The metadata accessed is limited to the following:

- Open Graph metadata via the page's `<meta>` tags (`og:title`, `og:image`,
  etc.)
- Twitter Card metadata via the page's `<meta>` tags (`twitter:title`,
  `twitter:image`, etc.)
- URLs of the page and the page's favicon (/favicon.ico)

No other data from the page -- including but not limited to the cookies, local
storage, session storage, or any other form of personally identifiable
information -- is accessed by the extension at any point.

## What data is stored by the Extension

The extension stores settings options (set by you), and the extension's own
state (the last selected preview tab, and the theme mode). This data is stored
locally on your device in your browser's storage, and is never transmitted to
any server or third party. No other data -- including but not limited to
personally identifiable information -- is stored at any point.

## What data is transmitted by the Extension

The extension performs a network request to fetch the image used in the previews
generated by the extension -- whose URL is retrieved from the page's metadata.
That's the only data transmitted by the extension, and is necessary for the
extension's core functionality. No personally identifiable information or user
data is sent to any server, third party, or any other destination.

## Changes to the Policy

Any changes to the policy will be logged in the Changelog section below, with a
